# Synthesis

This domain deals in the means of synthesis of tokens by a model for some end. An LLM is a computer algorithm capable of synthesizing tokenized output that encodes semantics; it is able to do so in the general sense by having its output tokens tuned by their priors. The workflows in this domain aim to manipulate, filter & transform this synthesis. We refer to `token` specifically as to not pin our selfs to any one medium.

## First Principles

### Human's construct Mental Models

The brain constructs a model of how its expectation of reality; it measures reality through observation & calculates its feedback error as the difference between reality as measure & it's model; humans extend that to higher order models of abstract thought & cognition itself.

### LLMs are Applied Information Theory

LLMs compress their training corpus into a high dimensional relational representation; LLMs are chaos theory engines generating one token at a time each manipulated by their priors; the goal of synthesis is resolving a useful signal from uncertainty; tokens are numerical representations of some target medium (text, vision, etc...)

### Context drives Synthesis

Session participants communicate through a shared "context" (information encoded in text); LLM synthesis can be calibrated with a PID controller; calibration emerges from ample germane context; minimal context is by nature uncalibrated; extraneous context leads to oscillating or divergent calibration
